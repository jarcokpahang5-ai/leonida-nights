Leonida Nights is an open-world crime game set in the sun-soaked city of Palmera Bay: explore the city on foot and by car, steal vehicles, outrun the police under a five-star wanted level, and work through six story missions for the fixer Vera Solano — with traffic, pedestrians, taxi fares, shops, a day/night cycle, and a live minimap.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 and press Enter to start. WASD moves or drives, the mouse aims, click shoots, E enters or steals a car, Shift sprints, Space is the handbrake, T picks up a taxi fare, and P pauses.
